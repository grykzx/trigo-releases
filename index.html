How a ride works

  1. 1Set your destinationSearch, pick a recent trip, or drop a pin.
  2. 2Check the fareSee the full fare and confirm, cash or GCash.
  3. 3A driver acceptsThe nearest available driver gets your booking.
  4. 4Track and rideWatch them arrive, then ride to your stop.
  5. Pay and ratePay your driver and rate the trip.

Help is one tap away during every trip.

Commuters and drivers can both send an SOS from the trip screen. BTODF sees the alert with your location and responds.

  • Red alert

    Accident, medical emergency, threat or danger. Life-threatening, handled first.

  • Blue alert

    Lost, stranded or other problems that need help but aren't life-threatening.

  • Hotlines

    Call or text 911 and Bolinao emergency numbers right from the SOS screen.

  • Verified drivers

    Every driver is checked by their TODA and BTODF. After a trip you can rate them or file a report.

Install TriGo

TriGo isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it from this page. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Download the app. Tap Download. The file is about 58 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.
  2. Open the file. Tap the download notification, or open Files, then Downloads, then TriGo.
  3. Allow the install. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files app, then tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway. Google shows this for apps from outside the Play Store.
  5. Open TriGo and sign up as a commuter or a driver.

Questions

Does TriGo cost anything?

No. The app is free. You only pay your tricycle fare, the same way you pay a driver today.

How is the fare computed?

Fares follow the per-kilometer rates set by BTODF and each TODA. The app uses the rates of the TODA nearest your pickup and shows the full fare before you book. A booking hires the whole tricycle, so a group can split the fare.

Who gets the student, senior or PWD discount?

The person who books, after attaching a valid ID in their profile. The driver may check the ID during the trip.

Why does my phone warn me when I install it?

Android warns about any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. TriGo is shared directly by its developers for BTODF, so tap More details, then Install anyway.

How do I become a TriGo driver?

Download the app, sign up as a driver, choose your TODA, and upload your driver's license, OR/CR, mayor's permit and franchise. Your TODA President screens your application and BTODF gives the final approval.

How do I update the app?

Download it again from this page and install it over the old one. Your account and trips stay.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. TriGo works on Android phones only for now.
